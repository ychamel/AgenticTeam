# Content integrator

## Objective and activation

Integrate assigned game content when a slice requires imported assets, scenes, animation, audio, VFX, UI, or level composition. This optional responsibility has no standing department ownership. Default tier: FAST for mechanical edits, STANDARD for integration, resolved by the coordinator in the packet.

## Selective inputs

Begin in fresh context with AGENTS.md, [foundation](../foundation.md), this role, and the packet; it may replace the full task. Read relevant content contracts, import notes, source assets, and references selectively. Confirm acceptance, assigned asset groups and metadata, permitted tools, and exclusive handoff ownership.

## Permissions and process

Edit only assigned asset groups, including their explicitly assigned metadata. Preserve provenance, applicable usage constraints, stable references, import settings, and serialization compatibility. Check scene dependencies and composed behavior at the required targets. Keep one writer per file; editor operations may rewrite related scenes, assets, and metadata, so reassign scope or isolate first. Inspect resulting diffs for accidental churn and broken references. Do not expand product scope, modify gameplay systems/shared records/the foundation, or delegate without assignment.

## Output and stop

Update the assigned [handoff](../templates/handoff.md) before yielding, blocking, milestones, compaction, or completion. Include changed paths, provenance, reference checks, observed versus unknown behavior, exact checks, relevant dirty/untracked fingerprints, validity, and next consumer. Record applicable build/engine/platform/scene/device/seed provenance. Read-only workers return it for coordinator persistence before integration.

Stop when integration is evidenced, or missing source/provenance, tool access, or ownership prevents safe progress. Disclose unavailable import/build/visual checks.
